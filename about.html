About Sydney

A Full-Service Broker, Rooted in Elmont

Sydney J. Sawyers founded Sawyers Premier Realty in 2014 with a simple goal: give Elmont and Nassau County clients the kind of direct, hands-on service that larger brokerages often can't offer. As a NYS Licensed Corporate Real Estate Broker, Sydney personally oversees every residential sale, commercial deal, rental, and investment transaction the firm handles.

Beyond real estate, Sydney is also a Certified Notary Public — one of the few in the immediate Elmont area — giving clients a single point of contact for both their transaction and the paperwork that comes with it.

Sawyers Premier Realty works across residential sales, commercial properties, investment and multi-family acquisitions, rentals, loan modification assistance, and notary services throughout Elmont, Valley Stream, Franklin Square, Floral Park, Hempstead, Nassau County, and Queens.
